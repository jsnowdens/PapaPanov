Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

Leo’s Toy Store LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates papa-panov.com to share information about Papa Panov’s Special Christmas. This policy explains what information we collect through the website, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

If you choose to follow Papa Panov, we collect the information you submit: your email address, name, optional ZIP code, and your consent to receive email. Our email provider may also record technical information associated with your signup, such as your IP address, approximate location, signup source, date, and time.

When you visit the site, our hosting and security provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and security signals. The embedded Vimeo player may use cookies or similar technologies and collect information about video loading and viewing.

How we use information

We use signup information to send occasional news about the film, screenings, availability, and related projects. We use technical information to deliver, secure, troubleshoot, and understand the operation of the website and video player.

Service providers

We use MailerLite to manage subscriptions and send email, Vimeo to host and display video, and Cloudflare to host, deliver, and protect the website. These providers process information on our behalf or under their own applicable privacy terms. Information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the website and mailing list, when you direct or authorize us to do so, or when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention

We retain subscriber information while you remain subscribed and for a reasonable period afterward when necessary to document consent, honor suppression requests, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Our providers may retain technical and security records according to their own retention practices.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any marketing email. You may also contact us to ask about accessing, correcting, or deleting your personal information, or to withdraw consent. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children’s privacy

This website and mailing list are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect personal information. No online service or method of transmission is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@papa-panov.com.
